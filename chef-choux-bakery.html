Our Story

Baked with Passion, Served with Heart

Chef Choux was born from a single dream — to fill every neighbourhood with the warm, buttery scent of freshly baked magic. Founded in 2012 by pastry chef Aditi Rao, our bakery blends classic French techniques with local flavours you'll instantly love.

1

Sourced with Care

We choose only the finest ingredients — farm-fresh eggs, premium butter, and seasonal fruits sourced from trusted local suppliers every morning.

2

Crafted by Hand

From laminating croissant dough to piping perfectly round choux puffs, every item is made by our skilled bakers who take immense pride in their craft.

3

Freshly Baked Daily

Our ovens start at 5 AM so that by the time you walk in, everything on the shelf is bakery-fresh, warm, and irresistible.

4

Delivered to You

Whether you visit us in-store or place a custom order, we ensure your treats arrive perfectly packaged and on time.
